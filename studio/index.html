    No songs yet. Add some to get started. Drop songs anywhere on the page to add them. To add lyrics to a song, add a .lrc file with the same name. Drag a song by its handle to change the order.

    Engine

    Rate

    How many times per second the picture is redrawn. Higher looks smoother but works the computer harder.

    Smoothness

    How many frames per second the computer draws now, next to its normal for this Rate. When it falls behind, a lower Rate or Quality, or the AI off, makes the page lighter. Measuring…

    Lyrics

    Shows the song's words on screen when the track has lyrics. To add lyrics, add a .lrc file with the same name as the song to the playlist.

    Lyrics lookup

    If a song has no lyrics, tries to find them on the internet by its file name.

    Shake

    How much the lyrics move along with the visuals. 0 keeps them still, 1 moves them as much as everything else.

    AI

    The AIs that listen to the song together with the visuals. Each one has its own job, and its settings are listed under it.

    Stem separation

    An AI listens to the song and splits it into instruments (drums, bass, vocals, guitar, keys and the rest), so the visuals can react to each one separately. It needs a browser with WebGPU. When it is off or unavailable, all instruments react together.

    Status

    How the AI runs on this computer.

    Model

    Which trained model splits the song. The number is the size of the model.

    Drums × 1

    Makes the drums stronger or weaker for the visuals. 1 leaves it exactly as the AI split it.

    Bass × 1

    Makes the bass stronger or weaker for the visuals. 1 leaves it exactly as the AI split it.

    Vocals × 1

    Makes the vocals stronger or weaker for the visuals. 1 leaves it exactly as the AI split it.

    Guitar × 1

    Makes the guitar stronger or weaker for the visuals. 1 leaves it exactly as the AI split it.

    Keys × 1

    Makes the keys stronger or weaker for the visuals. 1 leaves it exactly as the AI split it.

    Other × 1

    Makes everything else (strings, wind, pads…) stronger or weaker for the visuals. 1 leaves it exactly as the AI split it.

    Visualization

    Type

    The look of the visuals. Press ↑ / ↓ with the panels closed to switch.

    Quality

    How much detail the visuals pick up from the music. Higher is more detailed but works the computer harder. From 5 to 15.

    Smoothing

    How softly the visuals change. 0 reacts instantly and looks jumpy, 1 is the smoothest.

    Focus

    Which loudness the visuals pay most attention to. Lower it for quiet music, raise it for loud music.

    Spread

    How wide a range of loudness the visuals react to. Wider shows both quiet and loud parts, narrower makes the movement sharper.

    Boost

    Makes the music louder for the visuals only, not for your ears. Raise it when the visuals barely move. 1 leaves it unchanged.

    Tilt

    Shifts attention between low and high sounds. Higher favours cymbals and voices, lower favours bass and kick drums. 0 is balanced.

    Punch

    Evens out quiet and loud parts so beats stand out more. 0 is off.

    Layers

    The pieces this picture is made of, from top to bottom. Drag a layer by its handle to change the order. Lyrics always stay on top and the background at the bottom.
